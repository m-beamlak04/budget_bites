# AI Usage Log – Budget Bites

**Project:** Budget Bites – Mobile Development Project 1  
**AI Tool Used:** ChatGPT

## General AI Usage Disclosure

I used ChatGPT as a learning and support tool while working on Budget Bites. My main uses were understanding some Flutter and Dart concepts, organizing the project requirements, getting testing ideas, troubleshooting problems, and reviewing my code.

Since the project instructions were spread across multiple pages, I also used ChatGPT to help me keep track of what needed to be completed and what was already done.

I reviewed the explanations and suggestions, tested the app myself, and made decisions about what to keep or change. ChatGPT was used to support my learning and development process.

## AI Usage Entries

### Entry 1 – Project Planning and Organization
**Date:** October 15, 2026  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Prompt Summary:** Asked ChatGPT to help organize the Project 1 instructions, requirements, and development tasks.

**Output Used:** Checklists, explanations of requirements, and a step-by-step development plan.

**Verification:** Compared the checklist and explanations with the professor's project instructions and tracked my progress as I completed each part.

**Learning Reflection:** This helped me stay organized because the project information was spread across several HTML pages. It made it easier to focus on one requirement at a time without missing important tasks.

### Entry 2 – Understanding Flutter and Dart Concepts
**Date:** October 22, 2026  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Prompt Summary:** Asked for explanations of Flutter and Dart concepts I was unfamiliar with or confused about, such as `mounted`, callbacks, controllers, and nullable values.

**Output Used:** Simple explanations, examples, and walkthroughs of how these concepts would work in my app.

**Verification:** Reviewed the related code and checked how the concepts can be used in my app and how it helps me achieve my app's goal or purpose.

**Learning Reflection:** This helped me understand why certain parts of the code were necessary instead of only knowing what they did. For example, I learned why checking `mounted` matters when updating a screen after an asynchronous operation.

### Entry 3 – Implementation Guidance and Debugging
**Date:** October 2026 / Throughout the implementation & testing phase  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Prompt Summary:** Asked for help when I was stuck, confused by code, or encountered problems while implementing features.

**Output Used:** Debugging suggestions, code explanations, and guidance on possible fixes.

**Verification:** Reviewed the suggested changes, ran the app, and checked whether the affected features worked as expected.

**Learning Reflection:** This helped me understand what was causing certain problems and how to approach debugging instead of randomly changing code until something worked.

### Entry 4 – Testing and Finding Possible Issues
**Date:** October 2026 / Over the span of testing phase of the app  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Prompt Summary:** Asked ChatGPT to suggest different testing scenarios so I could check for problems I might not think of myself.

**Output Used:** Testing checklists and scenarios involving form validation, navigation, saving data, restarting the app, landscape orientation, and other user interactions.

**Verification:** Manually tested the suggested scenarios in the app and checked the actual results. I used the results to identify anything that needed attention.

**Learning Reflection:** This helped me think beyond whether a feature simply worked once. I learned to consider different inputs, screen conditions, and user actions that could reveal unexpected behavior.

### Entry 5 – Lunch Lens Smart Helper
**Date:** October 24, 2026  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Prompt Summary:** Asked for explanations and review of the Lunch Lens prediction logic, including how purchase history is used to estimate prices and compare them with the user's budget.

**Output Used:** Explanations of the prediction calculation, recent purchase history, baseline price, and affordability comparison.

**Verification:** Reviewed the predictor code and checked how the displayed prediction relates to the recorded purchase prices and saved budget.

**Learning Reflection:** I learned how a simple calculation using previous data can provide a useful estimate without needing a trained machine learning model. I also better understood how to explain the prediction to users.

## Final Reflection

Overall, ChatGPT was useful for learning, organization, troubleshooting, testing ideas, and reviewing my work. It helped me break down a larger project into smaller tasks and understand concepts that I initially found confusing. This helped a lot especially since I was doing this project alone and ChatGPT was used almost like my assistant partner.

One important thing I learned is that AI suggestions still need to be checked. Just because an explanation or code suggestion sounds correct does not mean it will work as expected in the actual app. I used testing and code review to check the results and made the final decisions about my project.
